# Q3-12: What is the difference between IServiceCollection and IServiceProvider ?

- IServiceCollection:

  - It's a collection (like a list) where you register your application's services.
  - You use it to define what services your app needs, and what their lifetimes are (singleton, scoped, transient).
  - It’s used during app startup.

```csharp
var services = new ServiceCollection();
services.AddSingleton<ILogger, ConsoleLogger>();
services.AddScoped<IOrderService, OrderService>();
```

- IServiceProvider:

  - It’s the object that resolves (creates and injects) your services when you need them.
  - After you register services in IServiceCollection, you build an IServiceProvider from it.

```csharp
var provider = services.BuildServiceProvider();
var logger = provider.GetService<ILogger>();
```
