## Q2-1: What is OOP?

- OOP is a programming paradigm based on the concept of objects. These objects bundle data (fields/properties) and behavior (methods) together.

- Key Principles of OOP:

    1. Encapsulation
    2. Abstraction
    3. Inheritance
    4. Polymorphism
