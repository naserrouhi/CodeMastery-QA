# Q2-86: What's the difference between Fanout messaging and Publisher-Subscriber messaging?

1. Fanout Messaging

    - A message is sent to multiple subscribers without filtering.
    - Broadcasting notifications (e.g., sending a system-wide alert to all users).

2. Publisher-Subscriber (Pub/Sub) Messaging

    - A message is sent to multiple subscribers, but subscribers receive only relevant messages based on topics or filters.
    - Consumers get messages based on interest (routing keys).
    - A news service where users subscribe to specific categories.
