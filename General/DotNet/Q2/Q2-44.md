# Q2-44: What's Checksum?

Cryptohgraphy, hash, digital signature?

- It is a small piece of data that is calculated from a larger set of data to check if the data has been corrupted or changed during transmission or storage.

- It is designed to detect accidental errors, such as those caused by noise during transmission, but they are not secure against intentional tampering.

-Steps:

  1. The sender calculates a checksum by applying a mathematical formula to the data. The checksum is then sent along with the data.

  2. The receiver also calculates the checksum for the received data and compares it with the checksum sent by the sender.

  3. If both checksums match, the data is considered intact. If they don't match, it means the data might have been corrupted, and the receiver will ask the sender to resend it.
